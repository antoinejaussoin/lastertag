# 3D-printing placeholder

No enclosure geometry lived here yet. The first printable gun shell is now in
[`projects/gun/cad/`](../../../gun/cad/): Glock 17–proportioned OpenSCAD sources,
STLs, and print notes for the Pico / OLED / mag-battery / IR LED packing.

This directory will still collect later shared parts:

- body receiver pods with a clear IR field of view;
- wearable harness clips and strain relief;
- revised gun revisions after field testing.

## Requirements to capture before CAD

- final PCB, battery, connector, switch, and screen dimensions;
- emitter field of view and optical alignment;
- receiver visibility and protection from direct impacts;
- hand sizes, ambidextrous use, mass, balance, and glove clearance;
- rounded edges, recessed fasteners, ventilation, and service access;
- rigid LiPo protection against bending, puncture, crushing, and direct impact;
- access to the power button and Micro-USB charging port without exposing the battery;
- cable snag prevention and breakaway behavior;
- printer tolerances, wall thickness, material, and assembly method.

Use temporary project boxes until these measurements are stable. A future
design review must cover mechanical safety and local rules for realistic
weapon-shaped objects before public use.
